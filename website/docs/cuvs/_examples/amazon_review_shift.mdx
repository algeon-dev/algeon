The query groups 99,539 sampled 2021 to 2022 headphone and earbud reviews using
prepared text vectors, then compares each product's share in each KMeans group
across the two years. Each denominator counts sampled reviews rated at most two
stars for that product and year.

The KMeans assignments join back to review dates, product titles and original
text. The linked example is the 2022 review with the smallest ID for its
product and group. It is not selected as representative text.

**Follow-up: check the charging explanation.** The 27.92-point change raises a
specific question about charging complaints. A raw-table query counts NanoPods
reviews containing "charg" in the title or body. It uses all 2021 to 2022 reviews
rated at most two stars, without the embedding sample, text-length filter or
verified-purchase condition.
