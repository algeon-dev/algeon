### Which travel headphones deserve a catalog review?

A catalog reviewer is looking for "Wireless noise cancelling over ear
headphones for long airplane flights." Eligible products cost $20 to $150 in the
snapshot, have at least 100 verified reviews in 2022 and have no more than
20% low ratings among those reviews.

SQL defines the eligible products before exact nearest-neighbor search, then
joins ranked IDs to titles and review counts. The request and product vectors
were encoded before the query. Both are L2 normalized; squared L2 distance
`d` converts to cosine similarity as `1 - d / 2`.

filters and satisfying the request:

### Which Sony listings should an editor compare?

For catalog maintenance, a separate query finds ten nearest listings for
each of 10,000 query products in a one-million-product corpus. An editor can
then compare condition, color and accessories before treating them as alternatives.

<CodeBlock language="sql">{capture.queries.catalog_batch.sql}</CodeBlock>

<p className="fineprint">The query took {timings.queries.amazon.catalog_batch.elapsed_s.toFixed(2)} s.</p>

The shared model name explains why these are useful comparison candidates.
Condition and bundle contents still determine the editing decision. The SQL
returns 100,000 matches and joins their titles in the same statement; it does
not establish that the pairs are duplicate SKUs.
