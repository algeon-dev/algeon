This example reads the raw `products` and `reviews` tables from the
[Amazon Reviews demo dataset](../../guides/datasets/amazon-reviews.mdx).
It does not use prepared text embeddings.

### Which review themes describe each group of products?

The saved query groups 2,384 products by eight keyword-mention rates in
their 2022 low-rating reviews. It returns the three largest average rates
for each of six groups. One group has the following profile:

| Rank | Issue mentioned | Mean product mention rate |
| ---: | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Durability | 35.99% |
| 2 | Sound | 18.61% |
| 3 | Returns | 11.23% |

Another group leads with sound at 45.10%, followed by charging at 24.20%
and connection at 24.09%. These rates suggest which original reviews an
analyst should read. They are averages of product-level rates; a product
with ten reviews has the same weight as a product with a thousand.

SQL filters headphones and accessories with at least ten low-rating reviews,
calculates eight substring rates for each product, and fits KMeans to those
rates. It then averages each group's product profiles. Top-K returns the
three largest dimensions within each profile, rather than across all groups.
No prepared embeddings or predefined complaint labels enter this statement.

Each rate counts reviews containing an English substring. Negation is not
resolved, and one review can mention several themes. Mentions of returns
do not establish a completed return transaction.
