The seed is *Attention is all you need*, and related reading is ranked by
shared references. Candidates are papers from 2017 to 2020 with at least ten
references and one of four catalog labels: natural language processing,
language model, machine translation or question answering. Papers with the
seed's title are excluded.

Jaccard divides shared references by the union of the reference lists. The
query gives cited papers negative reference-role IDs and citing papers
positive IDs, allowing an undirected Jaccard call to compare outgoing lists.
SQL filters the candidates and joins the returned scores to paper metadata.
