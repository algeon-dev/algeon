A trend analyst wants a shortlist of brands whose audience relationship to
Barbie changed between May to June and July to August 2023. A later check of product
names tests whether the change coincides with Barbie-related listings.

Each period has a graph of distinct user-brand likes. Personalized PageRank
starts from Barbie; ordinary PageRank supplies each brand's global graph
score. Their ratio, `lift`, describes relative graph affinity: 1 means the two
scores are equal. The query ranks the change in that ratio and requires at
least 1,000 fans per brand in both periods.

<CodeBlock language="sql" className="no-collapse">{capture.sql}</CodeBlock>

The first four rows are the largest increases. Taylor Swift is included as a
comparison for the product-name check below.

<tbody>{capture.records.filter((row, index) => index < 4 || row.brand_name === 'Taylor Swift').map(row => <tr key={row.brand_name}><td>{row.brand_name}</td><td>{row.lift_before.toFixed(2)}</td><td>{row.lift_after.toFixed(2)}</td><td>{row.lift_change.toFixed(2)}×</td></tr>)}</tbody>
</Table>

The statement combines the time filters, four graph calls and the brand
joins in one plan and ran in a median of 4.87 s over three runs. The result
identifies which records to investigate next; it does not attribute the
change to a product or claim sales impact.

**Follow-up: check product names.** The graph query did not search names.
This separate SQL counts like events on listings whose names contain
"Barbie", using the same periods. Its percentages describe listing-name
matches, while the earlier ratio describes graph scores.

Taylor Swift's graph score also increased, but only 135 of 164,499 later
likes were on matching listings. Its change remains unexplained by this check.
Name matches can include seller descriptions and color names; they do not
verify a partnership.

**Follow-up: change the seed.** A rise in graph affinity could reflect a
broad summer trend rather than Barbie. Running the same statement with
another seed brand tests that. Hot Wheels is a Mattel brand whose collector
audience overlaps Barbie's; Nike is a large brand with no July event. Only
the seed literal changes.

<Table>
<thead><tr><th>Brand</th><th>Barbie seed</th><th>Hot Wheels seed</th><th>Nike seed</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>{tieIns.map(name => <tr key={name}><td>{name}</td><td>{liftOf(capture.records, name)}</td><td>{liftOf(controls.hot_wheels.records, name)}</td><td>{liftOf(controls.nike.records, name)}</td></tr>)}</tbody>
</Table>

With Nike as the seed, none of the four brands appears among the 25 largest
changes. With Hot Wheels, NYX, ALDO and Primark appear with smaller changes
and AMC is absent, consistent with the Mattel collector audience sharing
part of the Barbie movement. The two controls separate the Barbie-specific
movement from a general summer rise; a seasonal baseline over several years
would need data this snapshot lacks. Item attributes are the latest retained
values, and the calendar comparison is not an event-date experiment.
