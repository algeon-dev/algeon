### How does BERT connect to the original LSTM paper?

Citation edges run from the citing paper to its reference. The query selects
BERT and LSTM by title and year, follows those edges with BFS, and joins the
path to titles.

### How many papers cite AlexNet within three steps?

The query counts papers at each distance from AlexNet without counting one
paper repeatedly. Reversing the edges follows papers that cite AlexNet and
then papers that cite those papers.

<CodeBlock language="sql">{capture.reach.sql}</CodeBlock>

<Table>
<thead><tr><th>Shortest distance from AlexNet</th><th>Papers</th><th>Mean publication year</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>{capture.reach.rows.slice(1).map(row => <tr key={row.distance}><td>{row.distance} {row.distance === 1 ? 'step' : 'steps'}</td><td>{row.papers.toLocaleString('en-US')}</td><td>{row.avg_year.toFixed(1)}</td></tr>)}</tbody>
</Table>

<p className="fineprint">The query took {timings.queries.citation.reach.elapsed_s.toFixed(2)} s.</p>

The three layers contain 151,243 papers, excluding AlexNet itself. Each paper
appears at its shortest distance.
