## Related analyses {/* #review-map */}

The [UMAP example](../../cuml/udtfs/cuml_umap.mdx?dataset=amazon-reviews#examples)
projects review vectors and joins the output to original text, allowing
inspection of review text alongside returned coordinates.

The [KMeans and Top-K example](../../cuvs/udtfs/cuvs_top_k.mdx?dataset=amazon-reviews#examples)
calculates eight keyword-mention rates from the converted reviews and groups
2,384 products by those rates. It returns the three largest average rates
within each of six product groups. Mentions highlight reviews for closer
reading, without requiring prepared vectors.

## Data scope

The source is [Amazon Reviews 2023](https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/),
Electronics. The saved preparation encoded{' '}
{capture.input_verification.products.rows.toLocaleString('en-US')} products:
one million form the search corpus, and 10,000 disjoint products are queries.
It also encoded {capture.input_verification.reviews.rows.toLocaleString('en-US')}
reviews rated at most two stars, with at least 40 body characters, from 2021
and 2022. BGE-M3 revision <code style={{whiteSpace: 'normal', overflowWrap: 'anywhere'}}>{capture.embedding.revision}</code> produced
the vectors outside SQL. The stored 1,024-dimensional Float32 values were
L2 normalized. The queries do not encode text during execution, and the
raw-text keyword query does not require this preparation. Prices and product
attributes are from the captured catalog.
