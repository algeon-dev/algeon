# Amazon Reviews

These analyses ask which products meet a search request and which changes in
reviews deserve investigation. SQL combines eligibility rules with vector
search, or joins review groups to dates and original text. The results below
include counterexamples that change how the scores should be interpreted.

The Electronics snapshot contains 43,886,944 reviews and 1,610,012 products.
Products and reviews join by `product_id`. The vector examples use prepared
1,024-dimensional BGE-M3 embeddings for a sampled million products and a
sampled million low-rating reviews. Text embedding was performed before SQL;
the queries shown here do not encode text during execution. Prices and product
attributes are from the captured catalog, and review counts do not measure
sales or product failure rates.

## Related analyses {/* #review-map */}

The [UMAP example](../../cuml/udtfs/cuml_umap.mdx?dataset=amazon-reviews#examples)
projects review vectors and joins the output to original text. It describes
the returned coordinates and the limits of interpreting distance in a projection.

The [KMeans and Top-K example](../../cuvs/udtfs/cuvs_top_k.mdx?dataset=amazon-reviews#examples)
calculates eight keyword-mention rates from the converted reviews and groups
2,384 products by those rates. It returns the three largest average rates
within each of six product groups. Mentions are prompts for reading reviews,
not verified complaint categories; this query does not need prepared vectors.

## Data scope

The source is [Amazon Reviews 2023](https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/),
Electronics. The saved preparation encoded{' '}
{capture.input_verification.products.rows.toLocaleString('en-US')} products:
one million form the search corpus, and 10,000 disjoint products are queries.
It also encoded {capture.input_verification.reviews.rows.toLocaleString('en-US')}
reviews rated at most two stars, with at least 40 body characters, from 2021
and 2022. BGE-M3 revision <code style={{whiteSpace: 'normal', overflowWrap: 'anywhere'}}>{capture.embedding.revision}</code> produced
the vectors outside SQL; the stored 1,024-dimensional Float32 values were
L2 normalized. The raw-text keyword query does not require this preparation.
