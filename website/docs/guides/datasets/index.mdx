# Demo Datasets

The examples use four public datasets. MerRec is the largest: eight
questions over 1.27 billion marketplace events, from the brands that moved
toward Barbie's audience to the candidate lists for a Pokemon campaign. The
other three pages apply the same pattern to a citation graph, a
product catalog and a malware benchmark. Each section states a question,
shows the SQL and its saved results, and explains what the composition
supports.

| Dataset | Domain | Size used by the examples | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| [MerRec](./merrec.mdx) | Mercari US marketplace events, May through October 2023 | 1.268B events, 82.6M items, 5.6M users | Barbie's July, shopping worlds and their bridges, a campaign list from graph, vectors and a model, lookalikes, a budgeted model, catalog duplicates, and a session-key check |
| [Citation Network](./citation-network.mdx) | DBLP / AMiner V12 papers, April 2020 snapshot | 4.9M papers, 45.6M citation edges | Research communities, a ForceAtlas2 neighborhood, shared references, and citation paths |
| [Amazon Reviews](./amazon-reviews.mdx) | Amazon Reviews 2023, Electronics | 43.9M reviews, 1.6M products; 1,024-dim BGE-M3 vectors for 1M products and 1M reviews | Travel-headphone shortlist, changing review groups, and original review text |
| [EMBER2024](./ember2024.mdx) | Malware benchmark feature vectors | 1.56M Win32 training files, 359,994 test files; 6,315 challenge files | Classifier errors, model contributions, and shared import patterns |
